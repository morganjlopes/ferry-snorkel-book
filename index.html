About this book

A gentle, funny read-aloud about wanting to see something new, and the friends who help you get there.

4+

Who it is for

Curious kids aged 4 to 7 and the grown-ups who read with them. Short sentences, big pictures, and one very loud sneeze.

5

Reading time

About five minutes aloud. Twelve pages you can tap, swipe, or flip with the arrow keys. Just right for bedtime.

♥

What it is about

Curiosity, and friends who notice when you are sad. Fern cannot do it alone, so the kids on board build, pack, and deliver a little help.

Meet the crew

One old ferry, three busy kids, and a bay full of fish.

Fern

A tired old ferry with creaky paint, sleepy eyes, and one big wish.

Mina, Theo and Jo

They ride every day. They are very good with sticky tape.

The fish

Zippy, twirly, and just as curious about what is up above.
